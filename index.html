TicketScanner

A personal tool. Not a product, not a service, and not open for sign-ups.

TicketScanner reads the concert-ticket confirmation emails in its author's household's own Gmail mailboxes, so that a private concert calendar knows which concerts have been attended and which tickets are held for concerts to come.

It is used by two people, on one computer at home. It asks for read-only access to Gmail, searches only for messages from ticket vendors, and keeps what it finds — the name of the concert, the venue and the date — in a database on that computer.

If you have arrived here from a Google sign-in screen and are not a member of that household, there is nothing here for you to sign in to.

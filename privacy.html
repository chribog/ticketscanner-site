Privacy policy

TicketScanner · last updated 20 September 2026

TicketScanner is a personal tool used by its author and the members of his household. It is not offered to anyone else. This page says what it does with the Google account data it is given access to.

What it accesses

With your consent, TicketScanner is granted read-only access to your Gmail mailbox (the gmail.readonly scope). It cannot send, delete, move or change any message. It searches for messages from a fixed list of ticket vendors and reads those messages to find concert tickets.

What it keeps

From a ticket email it keeps the name of the event, the venue, the date, and the identifier of the message it was found in, so that the same message is not read twice. It does not keep the content of your emails, and it does not read messages that are not from a ticket vendor.

Where it is kept

In a database file on the household's own computer, together with the access token Google issues. Nothing is uploaded to any server operated by the author or by anyone else.

Who it is shared with

Nobody. The data is not sold, not shared, not transferred to third parties, not used for advertising, and not used to train any machine-learning model. The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

How to withdraw access

At any time, at myaccount.google.com/permissions: remove TicketScanner and it can no longer read anything. To have what was already recorded deleted, ask the author.

Contact

Through an issue on this site's repository. Members of the household know where to find the author.
